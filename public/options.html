Custom words and phrases

One entry per line, up to 250 entries and 100 characters each. This editor shows original terms. Popup excerpts stay masked by default.

Matching ignores case, normalizes whitespace, curly apostrophes and Unicode hyphens, and respects word boundaries. “ass” does not match “class”. No regular expressions or automatic stemming.

Built-in terms

All six categories start enabled, including mild insults, anatomy and euphemisms. Uncheck unwanted terms or disable a category, then save. Matching is literal: harmless uses and names such as “balls”, “breast”, “cock”, “Dick” and “Fanny” can match too. Category buttons affect the whole category, including terms hidden by search.

Coverage is per episode

English-dub muting requires matching English dialogue/CC from the current episode version. Japanese audio stays unchanged. Unknown or unsupported audio is left unchanged. Select English subtitles in the player to filter their text when the renderer is supported.

When subtitles are off, audio filtering can continue only if the underlying matching CC data is accessible. CrunchyRoll Censoring never changes your subtitle preference. Missing CC, hardcoded subtitles, and unsupported renderers are reported in both the player and popup.
