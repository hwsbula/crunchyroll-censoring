# Chrome Web Store listing copy

## Name

CrunchyRoll Censoring

## Short description

Independent English profanity filter for Crunchyroll captions and matching English-dub CC audio. Coverage varies.

## Detailed description

Choose which English words and phrases are censored while watching Crunchyroll on desktop Chrome. This independent extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by Crunchyroll.

- English audio: silence complete caption cues containing enabled terms when matching English-dub dialogue/closed captions are available.
- Japanese audio: filter identified English subtitles while leaving Japanese audio unchanged.
- Non-English subtitle tracks remain unchanged.
- Review masked matches with timestamps, context, per-occurrence Ignore/Unignore and in-player seeking.
- Search 1,161 built-in words/phrases in six categories, enable or disable categories and add custom literal terms.
- Start with a simple popup; optional developer tools reveal caption diagnostics and manual timing controls.
- Carry preferences and word choices between installations with Chrome settings sync, or export/import a settings backup.

Read the separate audio and subtitle coverage statuses before watching. Some English dubs do not have matching captions: audio filtering is unavailable for those episodes. Whole-cue muting can also silence surrounding dialogue. Timing adjusts automatically for supported opening promos. Optional manual timing tools require independent verification and cannot solve changing drift. Hardcoded subtitles and unsupported renderers cannot be safely filtered. The vocabulary is broad, not exhaustive; some terms have harmless meanings. Filtering cannot cover every episode or expression; always check both coverage statuses.

Caption processing stays on your device. There are no ads, analytics, microphone recording, cloud transcription or extension account. When enabled, Chrome may sync your preferences and custom words through your Chrome account. Episode-specific Ignore exceptions, subtitle text and playback logs are not synced.
